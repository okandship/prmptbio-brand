The people and open-source work behind the lettering and tools used in prmpt.bio’s social media covers, posters and animations.

3D lettering

Pilowlava 3D was sculpted by Vincent Wagner, based on the Pilowlava typeface by Anton Moglia and Jérémy Landes, and published by Velvetyne Type Foundry.

Copyright © 2020 Vincent Wagner, Anton Moglia and Jérémy Landes. Licensed under the Free Art License 1.3.

This attribution covers prmpt.bio visuals using these 3D letters, including profile covers, social posts, posters and animated renders. Composition, materials, lighting and rendering are by prmpt.bio using its brand studio.

Adaptations for the brand studio

The prmpt.bio / okandship adaptation splits the original character set into individual OBJ files, re-indexes and translates vertices for layout, removes texture coordinates while retaining normals, and adds a glyph index with dimensions. The sculpted letter shapes are preserved. Smooth shading is applied when rendering.

These modified glyph files are distributed under the Free Art License 1.3. They may be copied, distributed and modified under its terms.

Brand artwork

The prmpt.bio key mark was generated with Recraft and subsequently edited for the brand studio, including opening the key’s bow into a true transparent hole. It is separate from the Pilowlava lettering.
